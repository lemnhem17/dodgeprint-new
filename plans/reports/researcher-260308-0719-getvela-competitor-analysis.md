# Getvela Competitor Analysis Report

**Date:** 2026-03-08 | **Report:** Getvela.com (Vela) Research

---

## Executive Summary

Vela is a multi-channel e-commerce listing management platform targeting Etsy, Shopify, eBay, and Faire sellers. Core strength: bulk editing + AI-powered photo optimization. Critical weakness: severe customer service + technical reliability issues reflected in 2.4-2.6/5 Trustpilot rating (86% 1-star reviews).

---

## Core Features

**Bulk Editing & Listing Management**
- Bulk update prices, titles, descriptions, tags across all connected shops
- Schedule updates for future deployment
- Cross-channel copy functionality

**AI Features**
- Listing scoring (ranks listings 0-100 based on SEO + platform guidelines)
- AI-powered photo editing: background removal, AI background generation, image expansion
- Photo studio for centralized image management
- AI copy optimization & listing merge tools

**Platform Support**
- Etsy (primary focus)
- Shopify
- eBay
- Faire
- Planning additional integrations (Pinterest mentioned in wishlist)

**Free Trial:** 7 days, all features, no credit card required.

---

## Pricing Model

**Plans:** Vela Lite ($29.95/mo) | Vela Plus ($39.95/mo)

**Scaling Structure** (per shop):
- 0-500 listings: $29.95 (Lite baseline)
- 501-1K: $34.95
- 1K-2.5K: $39.95
- 2.5K-5K: $44.95
- 5K-10K: $49.95-$54.95
- 10K+: Custom quote

**Discounts:** 20% off all shops when connecting multiple shops.

**Billing:** Monthly, per shop, based on listing count + plan tier. Issues: Users report charged for deactivated/expired listings; cancellation doesn't automatically stop billing (users claim continued charges post-cancellation).

---

## UX & Onboarding

**Positive:** Shopify app store ratings 4.5/5 (438 reviews). Users praise "clean, intuitive interface" + significant time savings on bulk editing vs. competitors.

**Negative:** Reports of "extreme long loading times" on profiles screen with 2K+ products; described as "sluggish in every aspect."

**Learning Curve:** Straightforward for Etsy sellers familiar with bulk editing tools; setup relatively simple.

---

## Differentiators vs. Competitors

1. **AI Listing Scoring:** Quantifies optimization potential; helps prioritize work.
2. **Photo Studio:** Dedicated AI image editor with background removal/generation (rare in category).
3. **Multi-Shop Discount:** 20% off for multi-channel sellers (pricing incentive).
4. **Free Trial Length:** 7 days full access beats most competitors.
5. **Shopify Integration:** Tight Shopify App Store presence (4.5/5 rating, strong Shopify seller base).

---

## Critical Weaknesses & Gaps

**Technical Issues**
- Data loss: Profile updates reported "permanently lost" all listing data without restore option
- Sync bugs: Images sync in wrong order; stock numbers don't upload; incomplete bulk edits
- Performance: Sluggish with large catalogs (2K+ products)
- Listings fail to post to platforms despite appearing complete in Vela

**Missing Features**
- No marketing automation (requires separate tools for campaigns, email, etc.)
- Limited platform coverage: Pinterest, Amazon, TikTok Shop not supported
- No print-on-demand integration (unlike Dodgeprint)
- No product research tools

**Customer Service**
- Described as "very rude and aggressive"; agents ignore support tickets for days
- Slow technical troubleshooting response
- One user reported 7 hours of lost work due to bugs with no resolution

**Billing & Cancellation**
- Customers report continued charges after cancellation
- Opaque billing (charged for deactivated listings counted against quota)
- Price increases reported as "staggering" ($50/mo for single-account users)

---

## Target Users

**Primary:** Etsy sellers with 500+ listings managing multiple marketplaces.
**Secondary:** Shopify merchants wanting bulk editing + photo optimization.
**Excluded:** Single-listing sellers; print-on-demand vendors; Amazon-only sellers.

## Dodgeprint Competitive Advantage Opportunities

1. **Reliability & Support:** Position as stable, responsive alternative. Vela's trust deficit is major vulnerability.
2. **Print-on-Demand:** Vela has zero POD support; Dodgeprint's POD + listing automation is unique gap-filler.
3. **Integrated Product Research:** Vela lacks research tools; Dodgeprint can differentiate with market intelligence.
4. **Transparent Billing:** Simple, predictable pricing vs. Vela's per-shop + listing count complexity.
5. **Customer Service:** Responsive support team is key differentiator given Vela's poor reputation.
